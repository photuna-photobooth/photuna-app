Your rights at a glance

  • 👁 Access — receive a copy of your personal data we hold
  • 🗑 Erasure — request deletion of your photos and session data
  • ✏️ Correction — fix inaccurate data we hold about you
  • 📦 Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • 🚫 Objection — object to certain types of processing
⏱ We respond within 30 days

Are you a booth guest?

If you used a Studio Photuna-powered photo booth at an event, we can locate your session using the event date, venue, and a description of your visit. Your photos are stored for the period shown at the booth (typically 7–365 days depending on the plan).

Contact the DPO directly

For urgent requests or to verify our identity, email our Data Protection Officer at dpo@studiophotuna.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (Philippines) or your local EU/UK supervisory authority.
